Graphic Designer Roadmap

A practical step-by-step learning path for becoming a strong graphic designer. Courses are ordered from fundamentals to professional strategy and delivery.

Level flow: Basics -> Intermediate -> Advanced -> Pro Total stages: 5 Format: Click course title to open source

1) Foundation: Visual Basics & Core Design Language

Goal: build core visual literacy and composition skills.

  • Basics of Visual Communication (3 cr)
  • Introduction into Typography (1 cr)
  • Visual Communication (5 cr)

2) Creative Practice: Ideation & Concept Development

Goal: generate original ideas and solve design problems creatively.

  • Diving into Radical Creativity (3 cr)
  • Creativity and Wellbeing 1 (1 cr)
  • Service Design Sprint (2 cr)

3) Digital Design: UX/UI & Web Fundamentals

Goal: expand from visual design into product and interface workflows.

  • Introduction to Digital Services (5 cr)
  • Basics of Web Development (4 cr)

4) Professional Growth: Brand, Communication & Strategy

Goal: design with business context and communicate professionally.

  • Brand Experience Development (5 cr)
  • Professional Communication (5 cr)
  • The Craft of Strategising (5 cr)

5) Pro Delivery: Teamwork, Time & Project Execution

Goal: deliver design work reliably in real project environments.

  • Teamwork and Project Management (5 cr)

Planning tip: Complete 1 stage every 4-6 weeks and build one portfolio artifact per stage (poster, brand concept, typography set, landing page mockup, or campaign kit).
