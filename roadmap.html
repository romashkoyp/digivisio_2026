Project Manager Roadmap

Follow this roadmap to build project management skills from basics to pro level. Courses are ordered by skill maturity, so you can plan your study time and resources effectively.

Level flow: Basics -> Intermediate -> Advanced -> Pro Total stages: 6 Format: Click any course title to open source

1) Foundation: PM Thinking & Communication

Goal: understand core PM role and build communication fundamentals.

  • Service Design and Productization (5 cr)
  • Effective Workplace Communication (5 cr)
  • The Art of Innovation Management (5 cr)

2) Discovery: Customer, Market & Product Definition

Goal: learn how to identify opportunities and shape product direction.

  • Competitive Advantage Through Customer Development (OPEN UNI) (5 cr)
  • Customer Insight (5 cr)
  • Customer Insight and Marketing (5 cr)
  • Strategic Management (5 cr)

3) Delivery Basics: Agile, UX & Engineering Collaboration

Goal: execute projects with teams and user-centric delivery methods.

  • Scrum Basics (3 cr)
  • Introduction to Digital Services (5 cr)
  • Service Design Sprint (2 cr)
  • Customer-oriented Developing and Innovation (4 cr)
  • Introduction to Software Engineering (5 cr)

4) Management Core: Stakeholders, Tools & Risk

Goal: run projects with proper planning, collaboration and control.

  • Collaboration and Negotiation Skills (4 cr)
  • Professional Project Management Fundamentals (Oracle Academy) (5 cr)
  • Basics of Organizational Safety, Security and Risk Management (5 cr)
  • Networks and Development (5 cr)

5) Advanced: Data, Growth & GTM Leadership

Goal: make strategic decisions with data and scale initiatives.

  • Data-driven Decision Making (5 cr)
  • Introduction to Data Analytics (5 cr)
  • Marketing Strategy and Practice (5 cr)
  • Growth Management in Enterprises (OPEN UNI) (5 cr)

6) Pro Level: Team Leadership & Future Skills

Goal: lead cross-functional teams and prepare for AI-enabled project environments.

  • Team Leadership (1 cr)
  • Elements of AI: Introduction to AI (2 cr)

Planning tip: Allocate 4-8 weeks per stage and complete one practical mini-project after each stage (for example: stakeholder map, backlog plan, risk register, or GTM outline).
