# Project Manager Learning Roadmap (Basics -> Pro)

Use this roadmap to plan your time and resources. Courses are ordered by evaluation level so you can progress from fundamentals to professional project management skills.

## Stage 1 - Foundation: PM Thinking & Communication
- Product Management (General): [Service Design and Productization](https://opin.fi/en/study-options/Humak/service-design-and-productization/c31a21e9-d1e9-4e7b-995a-63d7326a47ee) - 5 cr
- Communication Skills: [Effective Workplace Communication](https://opin.fi/en/study-options/KAMK/effective-workplace-communication/635dd652-4a73-4d2a-a41c-9dea2ab4cf3c) - 5 cr
- Idea Generation: [The Art of Innovation Management](https://opin.fi/en/study-options/Haaga-Helia/the-art-of-innovation-management/c15ba058-8823-4be2-87f6-10648786ba0c) - 5 cr

## Stage 2 - Discovery: Customer, Market & Product
- Product Identification: [Competitive Advantage Through Customer Development (OPEN UNI)](https://opin.fi/en/study-options/Oulu/competitive-advantage-through-customer-development-open-uni/6f7b9298-cfbe-4df8-a29c-e9701945f50e) - 5 cr
- User Research & Market Analysis: [Customer Insight](https://opin.fi/en/study-options/JAMK/customer-insight/816449e4-b711-4fbe-b694-87b55244e7dd) - 5 cr
- Market Segmentation & Personas: [Customer Insight and Marketing](https://opin.fi/en/study-options/Haaga-Helia/customer-insight-and-marketing/18ca1bab-c4a6-415e-9a89-6d5b8f767d5a) - 5 cr
- Product Strategy: [Strategic Management](https://opin.fi/en/study-options/Metropolia/strategic-management/e4060880-e6d5-48df-821b-9ad1b0d80d9a) - 5 cr

## Stage 3 - Delivery Basics: Agile, UX & Engineering
- Agile Methodology (Scrum Basics): [Scrum Basics](https://opin.fi/en/study-options/Metropolia/scrum-basics/80cf3154-04f6-42da-99e0-a0df342deb35) - 3 cr
- UX / UI Design: [Introduction to Digital Services](https://opin.fi/en/study-options/Haaga-Helia/introduction-to-digital-services/c0e0d3a6-d5fa-4f46-adbe-50dc3a4bbaed) - 5 cr
- Service Design: [Service Design Sprint](https://opin.fi/en/study-options/Metropolia/service-design-sprint/05e0298a-7264-4a00-b0b8-1b8cafb2d3fc) - 2 cr
- User Testing & Usability: [Customer-oriented Developing and Innovation](https://opin.fi/en/study-options/TAMK/customer-oriented-developing-and-innovation/29142f8c-b14d-4ea0-8ea2-58921fac7eb1) - 4 cr
- Working with Engineering Teams: [Introduction to Software Engineering](https://opin.fi/en/study-options/TUNI/introduction-to-software-engineering/40532bc8-c56b-4cf0-ad87-d687a1aa2b84) - 5 cr

## Stage 4 - Management Core: Stakeholders, Tools & Risk
- Stakeholder Management: [Collaboration and Negotiation Skills](https://opin.fi/en/study-options/Metropolia/collaboration-and-negotiation-skills/05c1523f-5fe3-48b9-a77b-aead33742a3c) - 4 cr
- Project Management Tools: [Professional Project Management Fundamentals with Oracle Academy](https://opin.fi/en/study-options/Metropolia/professional-project-management-fundamentals-with-oracle-academy/5fbe2632-d288-4ac8-b40e-eebb28bc4413) - 5 cr
- Risk Management: [Basics of Organizational Safety, Security and Risk Management](https://opin.fi/en/study-options/Laurea/basics-of-organizational-safety%2C-security-and-risk-management/e014b12d-6f85-438d-82d3-b429f0f45fd5) - 5 cr
- Managing Partnerships: [Networks and Development](https://opin.fi/en/study-options/Humak/networks-and-development/e5930e9e-682b-4d00-9928-742a8dc0b3d3) - 5 cr

## Stage 5 - Advanced: Data, Growth & Go-to-Market
- Data-Driven Decision Making: [Data-driven Decision Making](https://opin.fi/en/study-options/Laurea/data-driven-decision-making/3f0e22b0-23c1-48de-8305-7c0ae720d917) - 5 cr
- Analytics Tools: [Introduction to Data Analytics](https://opin.fi/en/study-options/XAMK/introduction-to-data-analytics/dab0e8e3-e7fd-4217-8713-92ca0edbad95) - 5 cr
- Go-to-Market Strategy: [Marketing Strategy and Practice](https://opin.fi/en/study-options/Metropolia/marketing-strategy-and-practice/7793df97-a9ee-4722-b35f-fdf7e66aa7f4) - 5 cr
- Scaling Products (Growth Strategies): [Growth Management in Enterprises (OPEN UNI)](https://opin.fi/en/study-options/Oulu/growth-management-in-enterprises-open-uni/903f94de-337c-4a89-af43-ac7003a867de) - 5 cr

## Stage 6 - Pro: Leadership & Future Skills
- Building and Leading Teams: [Team Leadership](https://opin.fi/en/study-options/Metropolia/team-leadership/34f76092-a606-40c7-93be-d9c98cdf6626) - 1 cr
- AI in Product Management: [Elements of AI: Introduction to AI](https://opin.fi/en/study-options/HY/elements-of-ai-introduction-to-ai/27d4b733-085e-4d4a-b98d-add0bff0b24d) - 2 cr

## Suggested Study Rhythm
- Complete 1 stage every 4 to 8 weeks.
- Reserve weekly time for one theory block and one practical project task.
- After each stage, create one concrete artifact (for example: stakeholder map, risk register, or delivery plan).
